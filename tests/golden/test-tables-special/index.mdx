## GFM table with pipes

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `a \| b` | logical or |
| `c & d` | logical and |
| `e < f` | less than |

<a id="gfm-table-with-html-ish-text"></a>

## GFM table with HTML-ish text

| Tag | Note |
|---|---|
| tag \<strong\> | keep literal |
| amp & co | ampersand |
